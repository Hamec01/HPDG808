HPDG 808

HamloProd bass instrument, version 1.1.0. Windows 10/11 x64, VST3 and Standalone.

New in 1.1: the Sound Library (search, filters, favorites), section presets, OSC B, Draw Wave, LOAD REF + AUTO DRAW and the SYNTH preview with PLAY and scrub. See the sections below.

Start

Add HPDG 808 to an instrument track and play MIDI notes. 808s sit best between A0 and C2. Choose a sound from the preset menu at the top (or step with < >): presets are grouped by genre, and each name tells its substyle and character, for example Dark Trap - Long Dark Grit.

Click the preset name to open the Sound Library: search by name, genre, category or character (Clean, Punch, Grit, Slide, Warm, Dark, Short ...), filter by genre, and press the star to keep a sound in Favorites. My presets lists the sounds you saved. Enter loads the selected sound and closes the library; < > at the top then steps through the filtered list.

Every tab has its own section preset menu (SYNTH PRESET, MIX PRESET ...): it loads only that part of the sound, for example a new envelope and tone on SYNTH while your sample, mix and master stay. SAVE AS stores the section as your own, RESET takes the section back to the loaded preset. 35 section presets are included.

PLAY sounds a note without a keyboard. Mode sets how notes join: Retrigger starts every note anew, Legato keeps the note going and changes its pitch, Glide slides to the next note over the Glide time (808 slides: overlap two notes in the piano roll).

SYNTH

Oscillator
Wave (Sine, Triangle, Saw, Square, Reese), Shape (its variation), Sub and Sub Octave (a sine under the wave).
Pitch
Punch: how far above the note the 808 starts, up to 48 semitones; Punch Time: how fast it falls to the note.
808 Tone
Drive, Even and Saturation colour the sound; Tone is a low-pass that follows the note; Tone Fall lets the upper harmonics fade faster than the body, as on a real string; Click adds a beater; Boost lifts the low end at the hit.
Envelope
Attack, Swell and Swell Time (after the hit the level starts lower and rises: the bloom of many recorded 808s), Hold, Decay, Sustain, Release.

SYNTH: preview, drawing and references

Preview
The strip at the top shows the last note you played, rendered with the current sound. PLAY repeats it from the orange cursor; click or drag the waveform to hear that moment. BPM (HOST when the DAW sends a tempo) and Snap (Off, 1/4 ... 1/32) set the beat grid.
Draw Wave
NEW PRESET starts from a flat cycle; drag across the grid to draw your own oscillator wave while a note plays. DRAW switches between your drawing and the Wave selector, UNDO and CLEAR work on the drawing. Mouse wheel zooms into the cycle (up to 32x), Shift + wheel moves along it, a double click resets the view. The drawing is saved with the preset and the project.
LOAD REF and AUTO DRAW
LOAD REF (or drop a file on the SYNTH screen) shows an 808 recording, up to 10 seconds, as a purple guide. AUTO DRAW in FULL rebuilds the whole recording as a row of wave frames with their own time, level and pitch: the sound keeps the reference's changing tone and length, plays on any key, and every frame can be redrawn (pick it in the Frame menu). CYCLE takes one wave cycle from the part you select. The reference itself is only a picture: it is not mixed into the sound or the export.

SAMPLE

LOAD an 808, a kick or any bass sample (WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3, OGG) or drop it on the screen. Set Start, End and Loop Start on the waveform. FOLLOW KEY plays it at the note's pitch; LOCK TIME keeps its length on every note; Stretch makes it longer or shorter; REVERSE and INVERT flip it. The sample and the synth play together.

MIX

The levels of the synth and the sample. Handoff lets the sample (a kick) play first and then crossfades into the synth: a kick that turns into an 808. OSC B adds harmonics 2 to 8 over the note (Level, Shape, Decay, Sustain): the 808 line stays readable on phones and laptops while the sub stays the same. Phantom adds the harmonics that make the bass audible on phones and laptops.

FILTER / FX

Filter: LP12, LP24, HP12, BP or Vowel, with Key tracking, its own envelope (Env, Env Decay) and an LFO (free or synced to the tempo). The HamloMag saturator: Classic, Tape, Tube, Iron, Germanium or Vinyl, with Drive, Harmonics, Even/Odd and Mix.

MASTER

The bass finished on its own, before it goes to the mix. The screen shows the response, the mono and the wide band, the dynamics curve and the stereo correlation.

Sub Filter
Cut removes the rumble under Sub Freq; Boost lifts it.
Band / Width
Under Freq the bass stays mono (Low Gain sets its level); above it, Width spreads it.
Stereo Harmonics
Adds harmonics (2nd / 3rd, Even / Odd or Crunchy) in the middle or on the sides; Dampen softens them.
Dynamics
Threshold, Amount (left compresses, right expands) and Release; LIMIT keeps the peaks under 0 dB.

Export and drag

EXPORT WAV saves the last note you played, as you hear it (with the master), as a stereo WAV; the save window opens in Documents\HamloProd\Bass808 Exports. DRAG: drag that note straight onto a track in your DAW or onto the desktop.

Your presets

SAVE stores the sound (and its sample, its drawing and its frames) in Documents\HamloProd\Bass808 Presets. They appear under My presets in the Sound Library, and in HPDG's Sub808 lane too. Projects and presets from 1.0 open and sound the same.

Questions

Not in the DAW's list: rescan VST3 plugins; HPDG 808 is in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3. Too loud or quiet: every factory preset is levelled to the same loudness; use Gain at the top. Contact: the support address on hamloprod.org.
