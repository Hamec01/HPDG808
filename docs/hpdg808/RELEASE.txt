HPDG 808 1.1.0
HamloProd bass instrument - Windows 10/11 x64 - VST3 and Standalone

HPDG 808 is the 808 and bass engine from HPDG, now as its own instrument.
Play it from any MIDI keyboard or piano roll, shape the sound, and drag the
finished bass one-shot straight into your DAW.

WHAT IS NEW IN 1.1.0
- Sound Library: click the preset name to search 212 sounds by name, genre,
  category and character, filter by genre, mark favorites with a star, and
  find your own saved sounds under My presets. < > steps through the
  filtered list.
- Section presets: every tab has its own preset menu that loads only that
  part of the sound (synth, sample, mix, filter / FX or master) and keeps
  the rest. 35 section presets included; SAVE AS stores your own, RESET
  goes back to the loaded preset.
- OSC B (MIX tab): harmonics 2 to 8 over the played note, with Level,
  Shape, Decay and Sustain. The 808 line stays readable on phones and
  laptops; the sub stays the same.
- Draw Wave (SYNTH tab): draw your own oscillator cycle with the mouse,
  zoom into it up to 32x, undo and clear. Saved with presets and projects.
- LOAD REF + AUTO DRAW: load an 808 recording (up to 10 s) as a guide.
  FULL rebuilds the whole recording as editable wave frames with its own
  pitch drop, tone changes and length, playable on any key; CYCLE takes
  one cycle from the part you select.
- SYNTH preview: PLAY repeats the last note from a cursor, click or drag
  the waveform to hear any moment, with a beat grid from the DAW tempo or
  your own BPM and Snap.
- 12 new Trap / Drill voicings made for OSC B: Clean, Punch, Grit and
  Slide, three of each.
- Projects and presets from 1.0 open and sound exactly as before.

1.0.2
- The 200 factory presets made again from a full fitting run over 1309 real
  808 recordings: each preset now sounds closer to the 808 it was made from
  (the median difference 22-33 % smaller in Boom Bap, Trap, Rap, Drum & Bass
  and Atmospheric, 10 % in Drill; Techno as before). Same names, same levels.

1.0.1
- MIX tab: LAYERS, WEIGHT and ENVELOPE now sit in two rows, so the Sustain
  and Release knobs are no longer cut off.

WHAT IS INSIDE
- 212 factory presets by genre and substyle: Boom Bap, Trap, Rap, Drill,
  Drum & Bass, Techno, Atmospheric, four utility subs and 12 Trap / Drill
  voicings; 35 section presets.
- An 808 synth: Sine, Triangle, Saw, Square and Reese waves, a sub oscillator,
  Punch (the pitch drop of an 808) up to 48 semitones, Drive / Even / Saturation,
  a key-tracked Tone, Tone Fall, Click and Boost.
- An envelope with Swell: the level can keep rising after the hit, as on many
  real 808 recordings.
- A sample layer: load your own 808 or kick, loop it, play it across the keys,
  or let it hand over to the synth after the kick.
- Filter (LP12, LP24, HP12, BP, Vowel) with its own envelope and an LFO synced to
  the tempo.
- The HamloMag saturator with six models: Classic, Tape, Tube, Iron, Germanium,
  Vinyl.
- MASTER: a finishing section for the bass alone - sub cut or boost, a mono low
  band with width above it, stereo harmonics that help the bass on small
  speakers, a compressor / expander and a limiter.
- Export WAV and Drag: the last note you played, with the master, as a
  48 kHz stereo WAV. Drag it onto a DAW track or save it.
- Play modes: Retrigger, Legato and Glide (808 slides).

REQUIREMENTS
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. A VST3 host for the plugin; the Standalone runs on
its own. No activation.

INSTALLED TO
VST3:        C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\HPDG 808.vst3
Standalone:  C:\Program Files\HamloProd\HPDG 808\HPDG 808.exe
Guide:       Start menu -> HamloProd -> HPDG 808 Guide

HPDG users: the same engine, presets and MASTER are inside HPDG's Sub808 lane,
and from HPDG 0.1.0 Beta 9 the lane's editor has everything new in 1.1.0.
Your own presets (Save) are shared by both, in Documents\HamloProd\Bass808 Presets.
