## Release block

Label: `UPDATE / 1.1.0 · <release date>`

Headline: **THE 808 FROM HPDG. NOW ON ITS OWN.**

Under it: The bass engine of HPDG as a separate instrument: 212 presets by genre and substyle, a mastering section
made for bass, and one-click export of the finished sound. Also inside HPDG's Sub808 lane.

Cards (label / title / text):

1. `01 / PRESETS` — **212 SOUNDS BY STYLE**
   Boom Bap, Trap, Rap, Drill, DnB, Techno and Atmospheric, each split into its substyles: Dusty, Jazzy, Dark Trap,
   Rage, Memphis, Neurofunk, Liquid, Peak Time and more. Clean, levelled, ready to play.

2. `02 / SOUND` — **MATCHED TO REAL 808s**
   The presets were fitted to over a thousand professional bass one-shots: the punch, the tone and the decay
   measured, not guessed.

3. `03 / SYNTH` — **AN 808 THAT MOVES**
   Punch up to 48 semitones, Drive and Saturation, a Tone that follows the note, Tone Fall for a natural decay and
   Swell for the bloom after the hit.

4. `04 / MASTER` — **FINISHED FOR THE MIX**
   Sub cut or boost, a mono low end with width above it, stereo harmonics for phones and laptops, a compressor and a
   limiter: the bass leaves the plugin ready.

5. `05 / SAMPLE` — **YOUR OWN 808 OR KICK**
   Load WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3 or OGG. Loop it, play it across the keys, or let a kick hand over to the 808.

6. `06 / EXPORT` — **DRAG THE BASS OUT**
   Play a note, then drag it onto a track or press Export WAV: a 48 kHz stereo one-shot with the master on it.

7. `07 / SATURATOR` — **SIX FLAVOURS OF DRIVE**
   Classic, Tape, Tube, Iron, Germanium and Vinyl, with harmonics and mix controls.

8. `08 / PLAY` — **SLIDES AND GLIDES**
   Retrigger, Legato and Glide modes for 808 slides, a filter with its own envelope and a tempo-synced LFO.

9. `09 / HPDG` — **ALSO INSIDE HPDG**
   HPDG owners get the same engine, presets and MASTER on the Sub808 lane, and share their saved presets with the
   plugin.

Links under the cards: `Download with your key ↗` · `Guide ↗`

## What's new block (new in 1.1.0; place it right after the release block)

Label: `WHAT'S NEW / 1.1.0`

Headline: **DRAW IT. REBUILD IT. FIND IT.**

Under it: Projects and presets from 1.0 open and sound exactly as before.

Cards:

1. `01 / LIBRARY` — **FIND THE SOUND FAST**
   Search 212 sounds by name, genre, category and character, filter by genre, star your favorites, and step
   through the filtered list with < >.

2. `02 / SECTIONS` — **SWAP ONE PART**
   Every tab has its own presets: load a new envelope, mix or master and keep the rest of your sound. 35 included,
   save your own.

3. `03 / OSC B` — **HEARD ON ANY SPEAKER**
   Harmonics 2 to 8 over the note with Level, Shape, Decay and Sustain: the 808 line reads on phones and laptops
   while the sub stays.

4. `04 / DRAW` — **DRAW YOUR OWN WAVE**
   Draw the oscillator cycle with the mouse, zoom in up to 32x, undo and clear. It saves with the preset and the
   project.

5. `05 / AUTO DRAW` — **REBUILD A REFERENCE 808**
   Load an 808 recording and AUTO DRAW turns it into editable wave frames with its own pitch drop, tone and length,
   playable on any key.

6. `06 / PREVIEW` — **PLAY AND SCRUB**
   PLAY repeats the rendered note from a cursor; click or drag the waveform to hear any moment, on a grid from the
   DAW tempo.

## Short description (store / social / release record)

HPDG 808: the 808 and bass engine from HPDG as its own VST3 and Standalone instrument for Windows. 212 presets in a
searchable Sound Library, an 808 synth with Punch, Tone Fall, Swell and OSC B harmonics, Draw Wave and AUTO DRAW
from a reference 808, a sample layer, the HamloMag saturator, a mastering section for bass and one-click export of
the finished one-shot.
